# One-minute product demo — no voiceover

## Format

- 50–60 seconds
- 1080 × 1350 (4:5)
- 30 fps, H.264 MP4
- Burned-in captions and title cards
- Licensed ambient/electronic background music around 95–110 BPM
- Keep important text away from all edges

Do not record until Demo Mode has been inspected frame by frame.

## Timeline

### 0–5 seconds — Hook

Black or neutral title card:

> Your inbox is a database of your digital life.

Second line:

> I asked Codex to map mine.

### 5–12 seconds — Problem

Show the Subscriptions / Newsletters switch and summary cards.

Overlay:

> Multiple Gmail accounts. Years of subscriptions. No clear map.

### 12–19 seconds — Method

Show a short prompt card, not Gmail message content:

> Analyze all connected Gmail accounts. Read-only.

Then:

> Organization → Stream → Account

### 19–31 seconds — Frequency

Open Frequency Ladder and click one band so the subscription cards filter.

Overlay:

> What emails me most often?

### 31–41 seconds — Portfolio

Switch to Portfolio and select a category.

Overlay:

> What does each inbox primarily represent?

### 41–52 seconds — Age & Why

Open Age & Why, select an age band, and open one fictionalized detail card.

Overlay sequence:

> How long have I received it?

> Why am I probably subscribed?

### 52–60 seconds — Close

Return to the dashboard overview.

Overlay:

> Inbox Intelligence for Codex

> Open-source skill on GitHub

Final question:

> What would you build with multiple connected Gmail accounts?

## Editing notes

- Use 0.25–0.4 second ease transitions, not flashy effects.
- Add subtle cursor emphasis on clicks.
- Keep each overlay under two lines.
- Lower music volume during dense on-screen text even without narration.
- Do not show Gmail addresses, sender evidence, browser profile details, notifications, task titles, or private Site URLs.
